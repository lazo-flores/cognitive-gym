Cognitive Gym

Train the mechanics before test day

Gamified cognitive assessments are learnable, and unfamiliar mechanics are what cost strong candidates the most. Each exercise below runs on a fixed countdown, the difficulty only ramps upward (as in the real tests), and you get a scorecard at the end. Expect it to feel hard the whole way through: in adaptive testing, that feeling means you are performing well.

Everything runs locally in your browser. Your answers are logged on your machine only, downloadable from the header.

Switch

Deduce the position code that transforms one row of symbols into the next. Higher levels chain several transformations and hide one code in the middle.


Digit

Fill the blanks so the equation holds. Each digit 1-9 at most once per equation, including the digits already shown. Levels progress from addition to mixed operations. Adjacent underlined cells form one multi-digit number.


Memory

Circles appear one at a time at random positions. Memorize the positions in order while solving symmetry and rotation questions in between, then click the positions back in order. More circles as you succeed.


Full Assessment

All three exercises back to back at full length (about 21 minutes), with one combined report at the end. Real assessments often let you choose the order, so choose yours.
